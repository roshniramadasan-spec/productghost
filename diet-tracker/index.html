    High Protein Phase — key rules

    🥚 Protein first

    • No meal without protein. Aim for 0.7–1 g protein per kg of ideal body weight.
    • WOW drink on 5 days (start with half a sachet if you get bloating). Quinoa Dosa on 3–5 days.
    • Eggs for breakfast on at least 4 days; egg whites allowed anytime (free food).
    • Chicken at lunch only (lean, low oil); fish at lunch or dinner.

    💧 Fluids

    • Minimum 3 litres of water daily.
    • At least 3 glasses of liquid daily: vegetable juice, buttermilk, lime water, tender coconut water or teatox.

    🍽️ Meals

    • Truweight Plate at lunch/dinner: 50% vegetables, 25% carbs, 25% protein.
    • Grain-free dinner on 3 days this phase (or 1–2 small thin 5–6″ chapattis + buttermilk with superfibre + soup + salad + dal).
    • Dinner light; 2–3 hour gap between dinner and sleep. Max 3.5 hours between meals.
    • Fruit as a standalone snack (morning/mid-morning), never right after a meal, and not after 6 pm.
    • Oil max 2–3 tsp/day, cold-pressed. Fibre 25–40 g/day.

    🌅 Daily rituals

    • Morning: Seeds cocktail, Tru Juice (10 ml, ideally early morning), probiotics.
    • Before meals: 1–1.5 tsp Tru Fibre in buttermilk or water (free food).
    • Night: Triphala with lukewarm water before bed.
    • First meal within 30–60 min of waking — fruit/seeds/nuts/juice, not tea or coffee.

    🔁 Cycle

    • 9 days of High Protein plans + 1 cheat day. Rotate Plans 1–3; Plan 4 is your weekly detox day.
    • Sleep 7–8 hours; exercise 30 min, 3–5× a week.
    • Track inches, not just kilos — muscle gain can hide fat loss on the scale.
