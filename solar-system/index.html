✨ Fun facts

    How big are the planets, really?

    These circles are drawn to true scale — Jupiter really is that much bigger than Mercury! And the Sun? It's so enormous that about 1,300,000 Earths could fit inside it. 🤯

    ☀️ This curve is the edge of the Sun at the same scale. It wouldn't even fit on 15 screens!

    How far away are they?

    Space is mostly... space! Distances below are to scale (sizes are not). Light from the Sun takes 8 minutes to reach Earth and over 4 hours to reach Neptune.
